# 6.5 kN LOX/ethanol doublet: gap report

Config: `configs/ethalox_6500N_doublet_2026-09-26.yaml`. The findings come from seven discipline reviews, each verified adversarially. Where reviews disagreed I ran the numbers myself: the L* rows below are repo `evaluate()` runs with ED_ACCEL=off and the corrected SMD.

## Why L* was missed, and what 1.4 m actually costs

**Root cause.** `spray.py:257` names Ingebo's impinging-jet report (NACA TN 4222) but runs a different correlation:
- It uses the crossflow formula from NACA TN 4087, whose constant 3.9 gives D30, and treats the result as D32.
- It feeds that formula the jets' closing speed.
- Against TN 4222's 16 measured heptane points it predicts a median of 0.47x the measured D32.

With drops that small, c* efficiency barely moved with L*: 0.9517 at L* 0.8 m, 0.9563 at 2.0 m. The optimizer had no reason to lengthen the chamber. No test in the repo reproduces TN 4222.

**What sets the floor:**
- Fuel reaches 95% vaporized 155.8 mm from the face.
- The march also has to start after the 8.0 mm impingement standoff plus breakup length, which the model computes but never subtracts.
- Together that makes L* ≥ 1.16 m a hard floor.

**Recommendation: 1.3 m.** Going from 1.3 to 1.4 m buys only +0.2% c*, so 1.4 m is not worth it. The published LOX/ethanol engines sit at 1.3 m (IPSA, Marchi EUCASS2025-402) to 2.0 m (TM65, Sakaki 2015).

**Lengthening the chamber does not fix the chug margin.** Chug is set by LOX drop size, not by L*. With the raw TN 4222 drop sizes the gain margin is 0.92 at L* 1.3 m (0.94 at 1.0 m). Only a drop-size measurement closes this; see H1.

## 4. Checked and fine

- **O/F 1.515:** sits at the c* peak (CEA 1724.0 m/s at 1.52). The ambient-Isp optimum at O/F 1.65 is only +0.4%.
- **Vaporization march:** an independent march agrees within 7–15%. The drop-size input is the error, not the march.
- **Mixing efficiency 0.959:** consistent with a stream-tube Rupe Em of 0.845.
- **Cd 0.793 at zero crossflow:** Rohde TN D-5467 gives 0.80–0.81; Lichtarowicz gives 0.79–0.80 at L/d 4.
- **Jets intact at impingement:** Grant-Middleman breakup 597 / 431 mm against 7.0 / 8.6 mm free jets.
- **No steady-state hydraulic flip:** hot-fire cavitation number 3.7–7.1 against K_crit 1.64.
- **dP/Pc:** 0.309 LOX / 0.30 fuel, above the 0.2 chug rule of thumb.
- **Free-jet L/d:** 4.3 / 5.8, near SP-8089's preferred 5–7.
- **Chug loop structure:** rebuilt independently, gain margin 2.139 against 2.140. The loop is right; its inputs are the problem.
- **Chug model sensitivities:**
  - Tank ullage compliance: <1% on gain margin.
  - Manifold liquid compliance: 0.2% of the injector admittance.
  - Distributed vs lumped lines: 1–4%.
  - Flight fuel run of 2.5 m: gain margin 1.89.
- **No feed-coupled buzz:** loop gain ≤0.27 from 150 Hz to 3 kHz. LOX-line acoustic modes are open-open at 3.19 / 6.39 kHz, 18% clear of 1T. The LOX ring m = 1 mode at 4.5–4.7 kHz is heavily damped.
- **Nozzle separation:** only below Pc 152 psia, so start and shutdown only.
- **Expansion ratio 4.83:** within 3 m of optimum apogee.
- **Nozzle efficiency ζ_n 0.95:** 1.0–3.3% conservative against a divergence × kinetics × boundary-layer build-up.
- **CEA and handcheck:** the CEA table covers the design point with no clamping. Handcheck matches to 0.4%. Pe 93.7 kPa against CEA equilibrium 94.09 kPa. Stale pre-CEA R and T move x95 by only 0.3%.
- **Throat heat flux:** Bartz 36.6 MW/m² against the model's 36.2. The graphite solver reproduces (0.708 vs 0.738 mm).
- **Barrel liner:** worst-case bondline 342 K. Material above 600 K reaches only 0.2–1.0 mm below the receding surface.
- **Steel sleeve:** hoop stress 45.7 MPa at 529 psig, a factor of 3.0 on 304 annealed yield (68.5 MPa at 1.5x proof). Axial 14.6 MPa.
- **Liner/sleeve thermal mismatch:** <0.02 mm.
- **Stand pressurant:** the single dome regulator and common manifold hold O/F common-mode (Pc moves 0.56 psi per psi of tank). Start sag is equal on both sides (1703–1718 psi/s).
- **Line inertia at start:** does not limit it. LOX reaches full flow in <1 ms, fuel in about 5 ms.
- **Stay time:** 1.435 ms. Chamber gas inventory 7.8 g (upper bound).
